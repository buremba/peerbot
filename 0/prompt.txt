# Identity simplification plan — review request

Repo: `/Users/burakemre/Code/lobu` (Bun workspace, `packages/*`). Read `AGENTS.md` first.

## Context / constraints

- **No customers yet. Backwards compatibility is explicitly NOT a concern.** Prefer deletion over shims.
- Stated goal: **simplify the system, not complicate it.** A proposal that adds a table, a subsystem, or a
  compatibility layer needs to justify itself against just deleting something instead.
- Long-term target: one identity graph. A human is one `$member` entity per org; every platform account
  (Slack, Telegram, X, Jira, Linear, GitHub, …) resolves onto that `$member` via `entity_identities`.

## Current data model (verify these claims — I may be wrong)

Two tables that both look "identity-ish" but do different jobs:

- **`entity_identities`** — `(organization_id, namespace, identifier) -> entity_id`, live-unique per org.
  Translates an *external string* into an entity. This is the only door from the outside world into the graph.
  Written by `packages/server/src/utils/entity-link-upsert.ts` and `packages/server/src/authz/access-graph.ts`.
- **`entity_relationships`** — `(from_entity_id, relationship_type_id, to_entity_id)`. Entity-to-entity edges.
  `member_of` edges are the **read ACL** for channel/repo recall.

Plus two things I believe are redundant:

- **`chat_user_identities`** — GLOBAL (no `organization_id`), PK `(platform, team_id, platform_user_id)` ->
  `lobu_user_id`. Written by `packages/server/src/lobu/stores/chat-identity.ts:60-77` and by the `/lobu link`
  slash-command flow. I traced its readers to RBAC role checks only; I believe authz never reads it.
- **identity-fact engine** — `packages/server/src/identity/` (capability registry, assurance levels,
  `IDENTITY_ENGINE_SHADOW` flag). I verified it never writes `entity_identities`; it writes `events` and
  `entity_relationships` (`engine.ts:429`) and is read via JOIN at `auth-hook.ts:90,149`.

## The bugs this plan is meant to fix

1. **SEVERE — permanent denial.** Adoption of a Slack identity onto a `$member` happens only at sign-in and only
   for Slack (`packages/server/src/auth/subject-identities.ts:315` gates on `providerId === "slack"`;
   `:100-141` is `adoptSlackIdentityOntoMember`). A human who signs in with Google/GitHub/Microsoft while being
   in Slack channels never gets their `slack_user_id` adopted onto their `$member`. Only email can bridge them,
   and Slack email is not currently fetched (see 3). Result: they are permanently denied Slack channel recall.
2. **Telegram / X / Discord mint nothing.** `packages/server/src/gateway/connections/sender-identity.ts` has
   `case 'slack'` and `default: return null`. So a Telegram user has no identity row at all and can never be
   linked to their `$member`. Email cannot bridge Telegram or X — neither platform exposes one.
3. **Hollow Slack person names.** `packages/connectors/src/slack-identity.ts:158-166` sets
   `name: combined` where `combined` is `"T09ABC:U123XYZ"`. Auto-created `person` entities are titled with the
   raw composite id. There is a resource name-refresh loop at `access-graph.ts:330-350` but **no member
   equivalent**, so a bad name is permanent.
4. **No Slack email.** `packages/connectors/src/slack.ts:41-62` — `SLACK_BOT_SCOPES` has `users:read`,
   `reactions:read`, `reactions:write` but NOT `users:read.email`. Also `member_joined_channel` is subscribed
   at `:68` with no handler.
5. **`person` <-> `$member` cannot be merged.** `packages/server/src/utils/entity-merge.ts:210-214` throws
   `cannot merge entities of different entity types`.
6. **`resolveRequesterMember`** (`packages/server/src/authz/channel-visibility.ts:154-164`) short-circuits on
   `byAuth`. I considered unioning the two resolvers and confirmed it does NOT work:
   `resolveRequesterByChatUserId` (`:100-140`) builds keys via `identity.buildUserKey(teamId, userId)` where
   `userId` must already be a *platform* user id, not an auth user id.

## Proposed plan

1. **Generalize `adoptSlackIdentityOntoMember` -> `adoptIdentityOntoMember(namespace, identifier)`.**
   Replace the Slack-only function; do not add alongside it. Its body already does
   INSERT -> adopt-from-`person` (UPDATE with `merged_from_entity_id = COALESCE(...)`) -> conflict.
2. **Call it from two places:** (a) sign-in, for *every* OAuth provider, not just Slack; (b) the account-linking
   flow. This is what closes bug 1.
3. **Repoint `/lobu link` to write `entity_identities`** instead of `chat_user_identities`.
4. **Set `merged_into` on drained `person` entities** so the old entity tombstones instead of lingering live.
5. **Add Telegram identity minting** in `sender-identity.ts` (namespace `telegram_user_id`), removing the
   `default: return null` dead end for the platforms we actually run.
6. **Delete `chat_user_identities`** and its store, after repointing readers to `$member` -> `auth_user_id`.
7. **Delete the identity-fact engine** (`packages/server/src/identity/`), including `IDENTITY_ENGINE_SHADOW`.
8. **Slack email chain:** add `users:read.email` scope, fetch via `users.list`, and declare `email` as an
   ADDITIONAL primary on `slackAclSource.memberIdentities`.
9. **Member name enrichment + a member name-refresh loop** mirroring `access-graph.ts:330-350`.
