## Decision: (b) — narrow `isSoftErrorResult`

**What settled it — the schema's own words.** `SdkScriptResultSchema` (`sdk_run.ts:68`) makes `success` a **required** field and documents `error` as *"Present when success=false: the thrown error, with script position"* — carrying `name`, `message`, `line`, `column`, `stack`. Those are *script* coordinates. A schema whose entire purpose is expressing failure-as-data is telling you failures here are **return values**, not call errors.

The asymmetry that makes this coherent: `run_sdk`/`query_sdk` **have** an `outputSchema`, so a script throw has a structured channel to travel through. `query_sql` has **none**, so `isError` is the only channel it has. That — not soft-vs-hard — is the real line.
