## What it is trying to do
`member_of` is simultaneously an ACL primitive and ordinary domain vocabulary. `ensureMemberOfType` upserts on (organization_id, slug) — it ADOPTS a pre-existing row rather than create-or-failing — so an org defining `member_of` in its own `lobu apply` schema would have that type silently promoted into the access path.

This PR adds a system-controlled `purpose='authorization'` classification, stamps it on the ACL type (repairing on adopt), and refuses caller mutation of any classified type.

Deliberately NOT in this PR: the three ACL read predicates (`AND rt.purpose='authorization'` in channel-visibility.ts, resource-visibility.ts, channel-messages-visibility.ts). They ship in a follow-up so a rolling deploy cannot have new readers demanding a stamp that old pods are not yet writing.
