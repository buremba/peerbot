entity write funnel has ZERO production bypasses. So the funnel comes FIRST, and
nothing downstream (approval/invariant separation, merge+unmerge as first-class
ops, then the hook) can start until this is nailed down. This audit is what
unblocks the whole Prodma ERP build order.
