         SELECT et.slug AS entity_type, e.organization_id
         FROM entities e
         JOIN entity_types et ON et.id = e.entity_type_id
         WHERE e.id = ${sourceEntityId}
           AND e.organization_id = ${ctx.organizationId}
           AND e.deleted_at IS NULL
         LIMIT 1
       `;

         action: "update",
         organizationId: ctx.organizationId,

   **The connector answer (corrected twice).** Final accurate position: `client.entities.link()`/`unlink()` DO exist (`reaction-client-types.ts:211-215`) but only in the **ReactionClient**, executed by `packages/server/src/watchers/reaction-executor.ts` — the Behavior engine. `ConnectorDefinition` has **zero** reaction hooks. `SyncResult` carries only events. So a connector emits events, full stop; entities and relationships require a Behavior in the loop. I also corrected my overstatement that this routes "through an LLM" — a reaction script is deterministic TypeScript in an isolated-vm; the LLM is the Behavior's analysis step. The underlying reason: connectors are modeled as **append-only observation** (events immutable, deduped on `origin_id`), while entities are **identity**, requiring resolution/merge decisions the connector layer deliberately doesn't own.
