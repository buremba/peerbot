Visibility is derived server-side by `resolveConnectionVisibility` (`packages/server/src/tools/admin/helpers/connection-helpers.ts:577-590`): personal `oauth_account` credential → always `private` even for admins; shared credential kinds → `org` only when the creator's role is admin/owner, else `private`.
